## Five Whys: currentness blocker

1. Why does the gate fail? Eleven records are past their review date or quarantined.
2. Why were they not renewed on 29 October? Their official pages could not be opened by the routes tried (ULII returned 403, `www.pdpc.go.tz` failed certificate checks, the ODPC link returned 404, Meta redirected to a login page).
3. Why did the failed route end the check? The register held one URL per record and no alternative official route, so an access failure was recorded as an evidence failure.
4. Why does that matter? A record that is only unreachable at one URL is a different state from one whose claim is unsupported; both read as `NOT_ASSESSED`.
5. Why not extend the dates? A date change would claim a review that did not happen. The fix is to find another official route to the same primary text (ULII's own PDF host, the PDPC documents path, the UCC-hosted PDF, the Meta transparency centre) and to record the route used.
