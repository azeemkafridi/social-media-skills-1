## Objective and measures

One job for the four weeks: move small-organisation finance staff from first sight of the product to a booked 20-minute walkthrough. Every measure names its platform's own counter and its denominator; counters from different platforms are not added together.

| Measure | Definition and denominator | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified profile or link visits | Visits from the three channels to the walkthrough page, divided by that channel's reach for the same days | Platform analytics plus UTM-tagged page sessions |
| Walkthrough requests | Completed request forms divided by landing-page sessions from the same UTM source | Landing-page form log |
| Opt-in rate (WhatsApp) | People who tap "message us" and confirm opt-in divided by people who tap | WhatsApp Business app counters |
| Substantive questions | Comments or messages that ask about a feature, price or data handling, counted per week | Inbox export, tagged by hand |

## Week 1 copy (ready for client review)

**LinkedIn post.** "An invoice record can pass through three people before anyone is sure it is complete. In a small organisation that usually means a late call to ask who has the missing page. Kite Ledger is built to hold the record, check the required fields and keep it ready for review. The demo below uses sample data. If you handle invoices for a small organisation, request a 20-minute walkthrough through the link on this page."

**Instagram carousel.** Card 1: "A record arrives. Who checks it?" Card 2: "Capture the invoice details in one place." Card 3: "Check the required fields before it is filed." Card 4: "Review the organised record when you need it." Caption: "A workflow is easier to trust when the next step is clear. This is a demo with sample data, not customer records. Link in bio to request a walkthrough." Alt text for each card describes the words on the card and states that the rows are sample data.

**WhatsApp greeting (Business app).** "Hello, you have reached Kite Ledger. Tell us what you would like to see and we will reply during office hours, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 East Africa Time. We only message people who have asked us to."
