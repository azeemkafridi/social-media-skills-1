# Uganda Advertising Standards 2019: clause screen

Read before releasing any ad, offer, testimonial, giveaway or influencer brief aimed at Ugandan audiences. It turns the UCC Advertising Standards 2019 (register `UG-UCC-ADS-2019`, status `partial`) into a screen a reviewer can run in ten minutes. It is a screening aid, not legal advice or a UCC clearance; counsel or UCC decide how the standard applies to a given post. Pair it with the [direct-marketing ethics filter](direct-marketing-ethics-filter.md) and the [legal and market release gate](../../../docs/quality-gates/legal-market-release-gate.md).

Basis: the UCC-hosted text of the standard was opened and read in full on 2026-10-31. Section and clause numbers below are the document's own (the standard numbers its main text 1) to 6) and then Annexes 1 to 26). Check the register entry for the current review date before relying on this file.

## What the standard does and does not say about digital media

| Question | Answer from the text | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Does any clause name the internet, social media, influencers or platforms such as Facebook, TikTok or WhatsApp? | No. Searching the extracted text for those terms finds none. | Read |
| Is there a medium-neutral hook? | Section 6 cl.12(a): advertisements must be clearly distinguishable as such "whatever the medium used". Section 3 cl.2 tests an advertisement by its probable impact on those who see or hear it, "the nature of the mediums" included. | Read |
| Does it reach campaigns that mix media? | Section 6 cl.21: a campaign that spans several media including broadcasting is subject to the standard, and enforcement covers all the media in it. | Read |
| Who is bound? | Advertisers, practitioners and media owners; the advertiser carries the principal responsibility (Section 3 cl.8). | Read |
| Do social posts fall within UCC's complaints jurisdiction? | The complaints section covers advertisements run on "platforms that it regulates" and the "Communications platform as defined in the Act". The Uganda Communications Act definition was not read. | `NOT_ASSESSED` |

Working rule: apply the clauses below as the house floor for every Ugandan campaign, including paid social and creator posts, and state in the review record that their binding force on a specific social post is unconfirmed. Do not tell a client that the standard "requires" an influencer label; say that cl.12(a) points the same way and the platform and ICC Code rules require it anyway.

## Clause screen

| # | Check | Clause | Action when it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every direct or implied claim has documentary evidence in hand before publication, current and relevant to the market | Section 6 cl.4(a)(i) and (ii) | Remove the claim or hold the post until the file exists |
| 2 | Survey-based claims were evaluated by an entity acceptable to the Commission at the advertiser's cost, and other evidence comes from or was assessed by an independent, credible expert | Section 6 cl.4(a)(iii) to (v) | Drop "research shows" wording or commission the evaluation |
| 3 | A claim drawn from a publication's research names that source in the advert | Section 6 cl.4(a)(vii) | Add the named source or cut the claim |
| 4 | No statement or visual misleads by implication, omission, ambiguity or exaggeration | Section 6 cl.4(b)(i) | Rewrite; list the implied claim in the review record |
| 5 | Competitor or price comparisons are factual, supportable and not disparaging | Section 6 cl.5 to 7 | Remove the comparison unless a dated, checkable basis is held |
| 6 | Testimonials and endorsements are genuine, based on the person's own experience over a reasonable period, and carry no efficacy claim the product cannot support; copies are kept for inspection | Section 6 cl.10(a) to (f); Annex 4 cl.43 and 44 | Replace with a real, consented testimonial; keep the consent file |
| 7 | A fictional character or actor is not framed as a real customer | Section 6 cl.10(e) | Label the portrayal or rewrite |
| 8 | Any living person shown or referred to has given express prior permission (crowd and background shots excepted if not defamatory or demeaning) | Section 6 cl.11 | Obtain written permission before use; also see the data-protection checks |
| 9 | The advertisement is clearly distinguishable as advertising whatever its form | Section 6 cl.12(a); Annex 4 cl.7; Annex 5 cl.1 and 2 | Add a plain "Ad" or "Paid partnership" label up front |
| 10 | The advertiser is identifiable where identity matters, and commercial intent is clear if the context hides it | Annex 4 cl.6 and 7 | Add the advertiser name and contact route |
| 11 | "Guarantee" and "warranty" describe a formal written undertaking with legal force and never reduce a customer's rights | Section 6 cl.14 | Reword colloquial promises or attach the written terms |
| 12 | A money-back or refund offer states its period and conditions in the advertisement | Section 6 cl.15(b) | Add the period and conditions |
| 13 | "New" and words implying it are used for six months only, first exposed within 180 days of the proven first use; "new" is not used for a change that is not material and cannot be substantiated | Section 6 cl.16(a) to (c) | Drop "new" from feature or launch copy older than the window |
| 14 | Content addressed to or likely to influence children (under 18; minors under 13; young people 13 to 17) does not risk mental, moral, physical or emotional harm and takes particular care for under-13s | Section 4 definitions; Section 6 cl.13(b); Annex 2 | Route to the sector annex and counsel; do not target the audience |
| 15 | Content does not play on fear without justification, support violence or crime, or discriminate | Section 6 cl.3(a) to (d) | Rewrite or drop |
| 16 | Taste and decency, and honesty towards consumers with less experience or knowledge | Section 6 cl.1 and 2 | Rewrite |

## Sector annexes: read the annex itself first

The standard carries separate annexes for alcohol (Annex 13), other specific products and services (14), weight control and slimming (15), gambling (16), betting services and tipsters (17, 18), lotteries (19), financial products, services and investment (20), charities (21), introduction and dating services (22), courses and educational opportunities (24), home-working schemes (25), contests (10), television product placement (11), faith and belief (12), and interactive advertising (8). This file does not summarise them. If the campaign touches one of these categories, open the annex, record the clause and mark any unread annex `NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Limits

- The standard dates from 2019 and says it is reviewed and amended from time to time. No amended edition is linked from the UCC page, but absence of an amendment is not proven; re-check the register date.
- It is a regulator's advertising standard; it sits beside the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (consent, direct-marketing objection), platform policies and, where relevant, the ICC Code, none of which it replaces.
- Fines, sanctions and the complaints procedure were not assessed here.
